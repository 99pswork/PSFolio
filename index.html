Questions Clients Ask

Straight answers on cost, timelines, Tally migration and who owns what — the things worth settling before a project starts.

What AI solutions do you offer?

I build AI agents and intelligent applications on LangChain, RAG pipelines and vector databases: WhatsApp lead-qualification agents, document processing, knowledge assistants and LLM features inside the tools your team already uses. Every agent is grounded in your own data, restricted to a defined scope, and hands off to a human the moment a question falls outside it.

Do you build custom ERP and billing software?

Yes. I build custom ERP, GST billing and order management systems around how your business actually trades: grade- and size-wise stock, weight-based rates, credit limits checked before an order is confirmed, and part-load dispatches. SteelERP is the flagship, and tailored ERPs are delivered module by module with the first one live early.

Can you migrate our data from Tally or Busy?

Yes. Masters, ledgers, stock items and opening balances move across. Fields are mapped with your accountant and balances reconciled before cutover. Migrate at a quarter- or year-end so you are not splitting a year across two systems, budget time for cleanup, and keep Tally read-only as a fallback initially.

How long does it take to go live with a custom ERP?

Plan in months, not weeks, for a full ERP, but you will see working software far earlier. I ship in two-week increments, so a first usable module, usually billing or stock, lands early and the rest follows while your team is already using it. What stretches timelines is messy legacy data, unclear internal approvals and adding scope mid-build.

Who owns the source code and the data once the project is done?

Ownership is agreed in writing before work starts. For custom builds I hand over source code, database and documentation on completion. Your business data stays yours throughout and is exportable in standard formats, so there is no lock-in. An NDA before you share process details is normal.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. I have delivered projects for clients in 11 countries, including the USA, Switzerland, Israel and Italy, working remotely with overlap on your hours. Most engagements start with a free 30-minute call, and I reply to enquiries within one working day.
